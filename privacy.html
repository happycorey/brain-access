Privacy policy

Corey's brain is a private tool used by one person, its owner, with the owner's own Google account. No one else can use it.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission it reads and organises the owner's own Gmail messages and Google Calendar events.

Where data goes

Data is used only on the owner's own computers, and by the AI assistant the owner runs there, to help the owner manage their own mail and calendar. It is not sold, not shared with other parties for their own use, and not used for advertising. The sign-in is stored only on the owner's computers.

Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove this tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
